## VPSDime example snapshot

`server-assets.json` and `server-pricing.json` are offline snapshots of the
resources embedded or referenced by the upstream
[`server.dart`](https://github.com/DavBfr/dart_pdf/blob/0833cd2fc8cf3e62b0228732e81402afca5cc542/demo/lib/examples/server.dart)
example. They preserve the upstream example without performing network I/O.
